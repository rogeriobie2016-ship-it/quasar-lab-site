Research + design lab · MIT 2018 · USC Media Arts + Practice

Become the brightest light.

A quasar is a galactic core around a black hole that outshines entire galaxies. The Quasar Lab merges film, music, data, AR/VR and critical pedagogy with people Not Designed For by existing systems, so that instead of being swallowed by the black hole of dreams deferred, they become the light.

    03 · Data Black Holes

    Invisible by design.Visible by design.

    A Data Black Hole is a data system that makes people disappear through bad design: the missing field, the box that does not fit, the dataset that never counted you.

    Each point of light is a person: a student, a parent, an artist, a neighbor.

    04 · Quasar Physics

    Stories and data on the same plane.

    Quasar Physics studies the x-factor of people who beat the odds. We put qualitative stories and quantitative data side by side, so neither one gets to erase the other.

      06 · Origin

      Born at MIT. Raised in community.

      Founded at MIT in 2018 and incubated at USC Media Arts + Practice, the lab designs for long-term social change, especially with youth. It works from the negative space between academia, nonprofits, government and industry.
